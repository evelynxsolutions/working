About Evelynx

Engineered in Kathmandu, built for the world.

We started with a simple premise: Nepali businesses deserve technology and creative work held to the same bar as anywhere else — without the overhead of hiring five different vendors to get it.

The Founder

Pusparaj Khadka

BIT · Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Evelynx Solutions was founded on a single premise: Nepali businesses shouldn't have to choose between quality and access. Every engagement — from a first-time e-commerce build to a full cloud migration — gets the same rigor we'd bring to a client ten times the size.

That means understanding the local market and regulatory landscape as well as the technology itself, and building solutions that work here, not just in a case study written for somewhere else.

What Drives Us
